== Character Creation Overview

Creating a character is a journey of both mechanics and imagination. Follow the steps below to build a unique adventurer who will take part in epic tales across the realms. Each section includes a reference to additional information to guide your choices.

=== Lineage
Select your character's *lineage*, which determines their ancestral heritage, traits, and abilities. Your lineage shapes your character’s innate magic, senses, resistances, and other unique features that will influence their journey.

link:https://d-ian-d.ian-m-m.com/races.html#aasimar_hmb[Choose your lineage →]

=== Class
Your class defines your character's role, capabilities, and special features they acquire as they grow in power. Whether you're a cunning rogue, a scholarly wizard, or a brilliant artificer, your class provides the foundation of your adventuring style.

link:https://d-ian-d.ian-m-m.com/classes.html#artificer_tce[Choose your class →]

=== Ability Scores
Use the *Point Buy* method to determine your character’s core attributes: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. These scores affect every aspect of your character’s performance, from spellcasting to combat prowess.

link:https://d-ian-d.ian-m-m.com/statgen.html#rolledl[Generate your ability scores →]

=== Background
Your character's *background* provides a glimpse into their past and grants proficiencies, languages, and other features that reflect their life before becoming an adventurer.

link:https://d-ian-d.ian-m-m.com/backgrounds.html#acolyte_phb[Choose your background →]

==== Ability Score Improvements
Assign your background’s ability score improvements. You may choose one of the following distributions:
 +2 to one ability score and +1 to another, or
 +1 to three different ability scores.

==== Skill Proficiencies
Select two skills in which your character is proficient. These represent areas where your character has received training or has natural aptitude.

==== Tool Proficiency
Choose one type of tool. Your character gains proficiency with this tool, allowing them to use it effectively.

==== Language
Select one additional language. Your character gains proficiency in this language, allowing them to read, write, and speak it fluently.

==== Feat
You may select one feat at 1st level, provided it meets the following criteria:
- It does not grant any ability score increases.
- You meet all prerequisites for the feat.
- It fits thematically with your character’s background and personal story.

=== Equipment
Start with the equipment provided by your chosen class. In addition, your character begins with *50 gold pieces* (gp) to spend on extra gear, supplies, or personal items.

=== Bio
Complete your character sheet with the following personal details:
- *Name*
- *Age*
- *Weight*
- *Height*
- *Physical Description*

These details help bring your character to life and guide how you roleplay them during your adventures.
