== Artificer - Infusions

=== Arcane Propulsion Armor
Prerequisite: 14th-level artificer Item: A suit of armor (requires attunement)

The wearer's walking speed increases by 5 feet.

The armor includes gauntlets, each of which is a magic melee weapon. They deal 1d8 force damage, have the thrown property (20/60 ft), and return after being thrown.

The armor can't be removed against the wearer's will.

If the wearer is missing limbs, the armor replaces them with functional equivalents.

=== Armor of Magical Strength
Item: A suit of armor (requires attunement)

This armor has 6 charges.

Spend 1 charge to add your Intelligence modifier to a Strength check or save.

Spend 1 charge as a reaction to avoid being knocked prone.

Regains 1d6 charges daily at dawn.

=== Boots of the Winding Path
Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer Item: A pair of boots (requires attunement)

You can teleport up to 15 feet as a bonus action to a space you previously occupied this turn.

=== Enhanced Arcane Focus
Item: A rod, staff, or wand (requires attunement)

+1 bonus to spell attack rolls.

Ignores half cover.

Bonus increases to +2 at 10th level.

=== Enhanced Defense
Item: A suit of armor or a shield

+1 bonus to AC.

Bonus increases to +2 at 10th level.

=== Enhanced Weapon
Item: A simple or martial weapon

+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

Bonus increases to +2 at 10th level.

=== Helm of Awareness
Prerequisite: 10th-level artificer Item: A helmet (requires attunement)

Advantage on initiative rolls.

Can't be surprised unless incapacitated.

=== Homunculus Servant
Item: A gem or crystal worth at least 100 gp

You create a magical homunculus servant using the infused item as its heart.

Shares your initiative but acts after you.

Uses Dodge unless commanded (bonus action).

If you’re incapacitated, it can act freely.

Regains 2d6 HP with mending.

Vanishes on death, leaving the heart.

==== Homunculus Servant Stat Block
Tiny construct

AC 13

HP = 1 + INT mod + artificer level (Hit Dice = artificer level d4s)

Speed: 20 ft., Fly 30 ft.

STR 4 (-3), DEX 15 (+2), CON 12 (+1), INT 10 (+0), WIS 10 (+0), CHA 7 (−2)

Saves: Dex +2 + PB

Skills: Perception +PB×2, Stealth +2 + PB

Damage Immunities: poison

Condition Immunities: exhaustion, poisoned

Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10 + PB×2

Languages: Understands languages you speak

Evasion: Avoid all or half damage on Dex saves.

Force Strike (Action): Ranged spell attack (range 30 ft.), 1d4 + PB force damage.

Channel Magic (Reaction): Deliver a touch spell within 120 ft.

=== Mind Sharpener
Item: A suit of armor or robes

4 charges.

When failing a concentration save, spend 1 charge to succeed.

Regains 1d4 charges daily at dawn.

=== Radiant Weapon
Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer Item: Simple or martial weapon (requires attunement)

+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

Bonus action: emit bright light (30 ft), dim light (30 ft).

4 charges. Reaction on hit: spend 1 charge to blind attacker (Con save vs. spell DC).

Regains 1d4 charges at dawn.

=== Repeating Shot
Item: Simple or martial ranged weapon (requires attunement)

+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

Ignores loading property.

Creates its own magical ammunition.

=== Replicate Magic Item
Replicate a magic item from a list based on artificer level. Each choice must be unique.

Common items and others listed in the Dungeon Master’s Guide or Xanathar’s Guide are valid.

=== Repulsion Shield
Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer Item: A shield (requires attunement)

+1 bonus to AC.

4 charges. On being hit by a melee attack, spend 1 charge to push attacker 15 ft.

Regains 1d4 charges at dawn.

=== Resistant Armor
Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer Item: A suit of armor (requires attunement)

Gain resistance to one damage type: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder.

=== Returning Weapon
Item: Thrown simple or martial weapon

+1 bonus to attack and damage.

Automatically returns to wielder after being thrown.

=== Spell-Refueling Ring
Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer Item: A ring (requires attunement)

Once per day, recover a 3rd-level or lower spell slot as an action.

== Druid - Animalistic Invocation

=== Pack Tactics
Prerequisite: None

You have advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the creature and isn't incapacitated.

=== Keen Smell
Prerequisite: None

You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

=== Keen Hearing
Prerequisite: None

You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.

=== Charge
Prerequisite: None

If you move at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hit it with an attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 4 (1d8) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your DC or be knocked prone.

=== Sure-Footed
Prerequisite: None

You have advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws against effects that would knock you prone.

=== Poisonous Bite
Prerequisite: The form requires piercing damage

On a hit, the target must make a Constitution saving throw against your DC, taking 5 (2d8) poison damage on a failed save, or half on a success.

=== Constrict
Prerequisite: None

On a hit, the target is grappled (escape DC 8 + your Wildshape Strength). While grappled, the target is restrained, and you can't constrict another target.

=== Amphibious
Prerequisite: Aquatic Form

You can breathe both air and water.

=== Blood Frenzy
Prerequisite: Aquatic Form

You have advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature that doesn't have all its hit points.

=== Relentless
Prerequisite: Aquatic Form

(Recharges after a Short or Long Rest) If damage would reduce you to 0 hit points, you are reduced to 1 instead.

=== Mimicry
Prerequisite: Aerial Form

You can mimic simple sounds. A creature can discern the imitations with a successful Wisdom (Insight) check against your DC.

=== Flyby
Prerequisite: Aerial Form

You don’t provoke opportunity attacks when flying out of an enemy's reach.

== Fighter - Arcane Shot Options

=== Banishing Shot
Use abjuration magic to temporarily banish your target to the Feywild. Target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be banished until the end of its next turn.

At 15th level, it also takes 2d6 force damage on hit.

=== Beguiling Shot
Adds 2d6 psychic damage. Target must make a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by an ally within 30 feet until the start of your next turn.

Damage increases to 4d6 at 15th level.

=== Bursting Shot
After a hit, the target and all other creatures within 10 feet take 2d6 force damage.

Damage increases to 4d6 at 15th level.

=== Enfeebling Shot
Adds 2d6 necrotic damage. Target must make a Constitution save or deal half damage with weapon attacks until the start of your next turn.

Damage increases to 4d6 at 15th level.

=== Grasping Shot
Adds 2d6 poison damage. Target’s speed is reduced by 10 ft. Takes 2d6 slashing damage if it moves without teleporting. Brambles last 1 minute or until reused.

Damage increases to 4d6 at 15th level.

=== Piercing Shot
Fires in a 30-foot line. Each creature must make a Dexterity save, taking damage as if hit, plus 1d6 piercing damage (or half on a save).

Damage increases to 2d6 at 15th level.

=== Seeking Shot
Choose a target seen within 1 minute. Shot curves toward target, bypasses cover. Target must make a Dexterity save or take full damage and reveal location. Half damage on a success.

Damage increases to 2d6 force at 15th level.

=== Shadow Shot
Adds 2d6 psychic damage. Target must succeed on a Wisdom save or be unable to see beyond 5 ft until start of your next turn.

Damage increases to 4d6 at 15th level.

== Fighter - Battle Maneuvers

=== Commander's Strike
Forego one attack to use a bonus action and command an ally to attack. Ally adds superiority die to damage.

=== Disarming Attack
Expend a superiority die to disarm a target on hit. Target makes a Strength save or drops held item.

=== Distracting Strike
Expend a superiority die on hit. Next attack against the target has advantage if made before your next turn.

=== Evasive Footwork
When you move, expend a superiority die to add the roll to your AC until you stop moving.

=== Feinting Attack
Bonus action to feint. Gain advantage on next attack this turn against one creature. Add superiority die to damage.

=== Goading Attack
Expend a superiority die on hit. Target makes a Wisdom save or has disadvantage on attacks against targets other than you until your next turn.

=== Lunging Attack
Expend a superiority die to increase reach by 5 ft on an attack. Add die to damage.

=== Maneuvering Attack
Expend a superiority die on hit. An ally who can see or hear you may move up to half speed without provoking opportunity attacks from the target.

=== Menacing Attack
Expend a superiority die on hit. Target must make a Wisdom save or be frightened until end of your next turn.

=== Parry
Use reaction when damaged by melee attack. Expend a superiority die to reduce damage by the die + your Dexterity modifier.

=== Precision Attack
Expend a superiority die to add to an attack roll, before or after the roll (but before effects are applied).

=== Pushing Attack
Expend a superiority die on hit. If target is Large or smaller, they must make a Strength save or be pushed up to 15 feet away.

=== Rally
Bonus action to expend a superiority die. An ally gains temp HP equal to the die + your Charisma modifier.

=== Riposte
Use reaction when a creature misses you with a melee attack. Make a melee attack and add superiority die to the damage.

=== Sweeping Attack
On melee hit, expend a superiority die to hit another creature within 5 ft of the target. If the same attack roll hits, deal damage equal to the superiority die.

=== Trip Attack
Expend a superiority die on hit. If target is Large or smaller, they must make a Strength save or be knocked prone.

=== Ambush
When rolling Stealth or initiative, expend a superiority die and add the die to the roll.

=== Bait and Switch
Switch places with an ally within 5 ft using 5 ft of movement. Roll a superiority die; you or the ally gains that much AC until your next turn.

=== Brace
When a creature enters your reach, use reaction and expend a superiority die to make a melee weapon attack. Add die to damage.

=== Commanding Presence
Expend a superiority die to add it to a Charisma (Intimidation, Performance, or Persuasion) check.

=== Grappling Strike
After hitting with a melee attack, expend a superiority die to attempt a grapple as a bonus action. Add die to the Athletics check.

=== Quick Toss
As a bonus action, expend a superiority die to throw a weapon with the thrown property. Draw weapon as part of the attack. Add die to damage.

=== Tactical Assessment
Expend a superiority die to add to an Intelligence (Investigation, History) or Wisdom (Insight) check.

== Fighter - Weapon Masteries

=== Cleave
Prerequisite: Melee weapon with Heavy property Make a second attack on a different creature within 5 ft. No ability modifier added to this extra attack.

=== Flex
Prerequisite: Versatile weaponDeal the weapon’s versatile damage even with one hand.

=== Graze
Prerequisite: Melee weapon with Heavy property On a miss, deal damage equal to your ability modifier.

=== Nick
Prerequisite: Light weapon Make the Light weapon's extra attack as part of the Attack action instead of a bonus action.

=== Push
Prerequisite: Heavy, Two-Handed, or Versatile weapon On hit, push a creature up to 5 feet away if it is no more than one size larger than you.

=== Sap
Prerequisite: Weapon with no propertiesOn hit, the target has disadvantage on its next attack roll before your next turn.

=== Slow
Prerequisite: NoneOn hit, reduce target’s speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

=== Topple
Prerequisite: Heavy, Reach, or Versatile weapon On hit, force the target to make a Constitution saving throw (DC = 8 + PB + ability mod). On failure, the target falls prone.

=== Vex
Prerequisite: Ammunition, Finesse, or Light weapon On hit, gain advantage on your next attack roll against the creature before your next turn.

== Sorcerer - Metamagics

=== Autonomous Spell
Cost: 13 Spell Points You can cast a concentration spell without needing to concentrate. Only one autonomous spell can be active at a time.

=== Careful Spell
Cost: 3 Spell Points Protect a number of creatures up to your Charisma modifier from your spell’s effects. They automatically succeed on the saving throw and take no damage on a success.

=== Distant Spell
Cost: 1 Spell Point Extend range of a spell by 30 feet per Sorcerer level or make a touch spell reach 30 feet.

=== Empowered Spell
Cost: 5 Spell Points Deal maximum damage with a spell you cast.

=== Extended Spell
Cost: 1 Spell Point per doublingDouble the duration of a spell with 1-minute duration or longer. Max duration: 24 hours. Gain advantage on Concentration saves.

=== Heightened Spell
Cost: 5 Spell Points Give one target disadvantage on saving throws against a spell.

=== Quickened Spell
Cost: 3 Spell Points Change casting time of a spell from action to bonus action. You can’t cast another leveled spell this turn.

=== Seeking Spell
Cost: 5 Spell Points Automatically hit a target with a spell attack.

=== Subtle Spell
Cost: 1 Spell Point Cast a spell without Verbal, Somatic, or Material components (unless consumed).

=== Transmuted Spell
Cost: 1 Spell Point Change spell damage type among: acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison, thunder.

=== Twinned Spell
Cost: Spell level (or 1 point for cantrips)When casting a single-target spell, target a second creature in range.
